## Full description (4000 char limit, identical on both stores)

Coin Odyssey turns the coins in your drawer into a collection you understand.

Photograph a coin and Coin Odyssey identifies it — denomination, year, mint mark, country, and an estimated grade — then writes a short history of the piece in your hand. Where it was struck, who designed it, what was happening the year it was made, and what a collector should look for.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Scan a coin with your camera and let it fill in the details, or enter everything yourself and still get the history.

Keep a proper catalog. Year, mint mark, denomination, grade, country, series, and your own notes, with photos of both sides.

Fill albums. Digital checklists for the 50 State Quarters, America the Beautiful, Lincoln cents, and world coins show you what you have and what is still missing.

Find anything. Filter by country, year, or grade, and sort your collection however you like.

Collect anywhere. Add coins with no signal and they sync automatically once you are back online.

BUILT AROUND LEARNING, NOT PRICES

Coin Odyssey does not estimate what your collection is worth. There are no market valuations and no price charts. The interesting question is what a coin is, not what it would fetch — so the app leads with the span of history your collection covers, and every coin arrives with its story.

YOUR COLLECTION IS YOURS

Your coins are private to your account and sync securely to the cloud. You can delete an individual coin, or your entire account, from inside the app at any time.

GOOD TO KNOW

Coin recognition includes 50 scans per month. Grades are estimates produced from your photos, not professional certification — for a formal grade, use a grading service.

Questions or ideas: support@mosaicstudioapps.com

## Claims deliberately avoided

- **PCGS integration** — never built.
- **End-to-end encryption** — untrue, and contradicted by the published privacy
  policy, which says encryption in transit.
- **Market value, portfolio tracking, price analytics** — removed by the
  education-first decision; also conflicts with the "no financial features"
  declaration filed with Google Play.
- **Cross-platform sync with the web app** — the web app is retired.
- **Exportable reports** — not implemented.
- **Guaranteed grading accuracy** — grades are AI estimates and the copy says so.
